01

A Pâtisserie Beyond
Borders

The Café began as an extension of the Restaurant's vision — bringing the same obsession with quality to a more intimate, everyday setting. From its first location in Beirut, it quickly became a destination for those who appreciate the finer things in life: handcrafted pastries, artisanal coffee, and dishes that elevate the ordinary into the extraordinary.

Today, over 15 locations span from Beirut to London, each designed to feel like a living room — warm, inviting, and effortlessly elegant.

Mireille Hayek Founder

Beirut London Doha Muscat Cairo Amman Riyadh Jeddah Kuwait
02

Crafted with
Intention

Every pastry is a miniature work of art. Every coffee is brewed to perfection. Every dish tells a story. Our pâtisserie chefs train in Paris, our baristas in Melbourne, and our chefs in Beirut — bringing the best of global culinary traditions to your table.

"The café is where luxury becomes accessible — where a simple cup of coffee becomes a ritual of beauty."

— Mireille Hayek
