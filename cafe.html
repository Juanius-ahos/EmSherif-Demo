01 Our Story

A Quaint Retreat, All Day Long

A haven for all-day dining rooted in family traditions, blending timeless Lebanese flavors with a contemporary flair. From Beirut to Paris, London, and the Gulf, the cafe is a celebration of warmth, connection, and the joy of sharing a meal.

Whether you begin your morning with a freshly baked man'oushe or unwind over an afternoon coffee, every moment is crafted to feel like home.

"I want this meal to remind you of the ones shared around my own table. Welcome home."
Mireille Hayek Founder
