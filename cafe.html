01

A Quaint Retreat,
All Day Long

Em Sherif Café is an all-day haven inspired by family traditions and Lebanese flavors, embracing a contemporary flair and spirit. In its cozy embrace, guests indulge in moments of simplicity and gatherings, relishing dishes that pay homage to the rich tapestry of Lebanese cuisine.

From its first home in Beirut, the Café now welcomes guests across the world, from Paris and London to the Gulf.

Mireille Hayek Founder

Beirut London Doha Muscat Cairo Amman Riyadh Paris Kuwait
02

Crafted with
Intention

Creations inspired by family recipes and the generosity of the Lebanese table, prepared with care and served with warmth. From man'oushe fresh from the oven to mezze, the grill and sweet endings, every dish carries the Em Sherif touch.

"I want this meal to remind you of the ones shared around my own table. Welcome home."

— Mireille Hayek, Founder
