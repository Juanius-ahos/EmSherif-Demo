01 Our Story

A Quaint Retreat,
All Day Long

An all-day haven inspired by family traditions and a contemporary flair. From our first home in Beirut, the spirit of the Cafe has traveled to Paris, London, and across the Gulf — a place where every visit feels like coming back to the family table.

Whether for a morning coffee, a leisurely lunch, or an evening gathering, the Cafe offers a warm retreat that captures the essence of Lebanese hospitality with a modern sensibility.

"I want this meal to remind you of the ones shared around my own table. Welcome home."
Mireille Hayek Founder
