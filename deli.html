01 Our Story

The Newest Chapter

A modern interpretation of Lebanese comfort, offering ready-to-eat favorites and Deli-exclusive dishes. From hearty breakfasts and artisan sandwiches to the signature Mouneh pantry and curated Matté and Arak selections.

The first Deli opened at Harrods Food Hall in January 2022, followed by the Beirut Starco location in March 2023 — each bringing the warmth of Lebanese hospitality to a new generation of food lovers.
