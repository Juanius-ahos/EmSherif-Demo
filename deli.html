01 The Story

The Art of Everyday Dining

The Deli was born from a simple belief: that extraordinary food shouldn't be reserved for special occasions. Drawing from decades of culinary expertise, our deli curates the finest ingredients and ready-to-enjoy dishes that bring restaurant-quality dining into your home.

From hand-selected olive oils and house-made preserves to freshly prepared mezze platters and signature desserts, every item tells the story of Lebanese craftsmanship.

02 The Philosophy

From Our Kitchen
to Yours

Every product in our deli is either made in-house or carefully sourced from trusted artisan producers. We believe in transparency, quality, and the kind of attention to detail that transforms ingredients into experiences.

"The deli is my love letter to the everyday — proof that beauty and flavor belong in every moment, not just the special ones."

Mireille Hayek Founder
