Our Story

A Table Set with Love, for the World

Step into a realm of culinary splendor, where Lebanese traditions unfold with a timeless Em Sherif touch and flair. Each dish is a meticulous tribute to the art of Lebanese dining, honoring a rich culinary heritage with the finest flavors.

What began in Beirut in 2011 as one woman’s table has become a family of experiences carried across continents, without ever losing the warmth of home.

“I wanted to recreate the table of my mother, where no one ever leaves hungry, and every guest is treated like family.”

Mireille HayekFounder & Culinary Director
