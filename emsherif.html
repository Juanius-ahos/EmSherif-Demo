Our Story

A Table Set with Love, for the World

Step into a realm of culinary splendor, where Lebanese traditions unfold with a timeless Em Sherif touch and flair. Each dish is a meticulous tribute to the art of Lebanese dining, made with intention and served with pride.

Founded in Beirut in 2011 by Mireille Hayek, Em Sherif was born from a wish to recreate the meals shared around her own dinner table, and to carry that warmth across the world.

“I want this meal to remind you of the ones shared around my own table. Welcome home.”

Mireille HayekFounder
