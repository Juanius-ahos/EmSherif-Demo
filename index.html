I Our Story

A table set with love, for the world

Step into a realm of culinary splendor, where Lebanese traditions unfold with a timeless touch and flair. Each dish is a meticulously crafted tribute to the art of Lebanese dining, made with intention and served with pride.

Savor the finest flavors that pay homage to the rich culinary heritage of Lebanon and immerse yourself in our uniquely created ambiance to create an unforgettable dining experience—where every moment is a celebration of culinary heritage.

“My passion for cooking is the secret to my success. I want this meal to remind you of the ones shared around my own table.”

II The Table

A feast without end

There is no ordering at Em Sherif. You take your seat, and the table begins to fill, dish after dish, cold and hot mezze in generous succession, until generosity itself becomes the meal.

Hummus and mutabbal, hand-rolled vine leaves, the finest Lebanese tartar, the grill, and sweet endings, all served to share, the way a Lebanese table has always been.
