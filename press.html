Our Journey

A Timeline of
Milestones

1995

First Restaurant Opens

Beirut, Lebanon — A vision becomes reality with the opening of the flagship restaurant in Downtown Beirut, redefining Lebanese fine dining.

2005

Expansion to London

Harrods partnership begins — bringing Lebanese cuisine to the heart of Knightsbridge with a dedicated restaurant space inside the iconic department store.

2010

MENA's 50 Best — First Appearance

First appearance at No. 12 on MENA's 50 Best Restaurants list, marking international recognition of Lebanese culinary excellence.

2015

Forbes 30 Under 30

Yasmina Hayek recognized on Forbes 30 Under 30 list for her innovative approach to modern Lebanese cuisine and leadership in the culinary world.

2018

Cafe Concept Launched

5 locations in first year — A Parisian-inspired pâtisserie meets Lebanese warmth, expanding the brand into a new dining category.

2020

Deli Concept Launched

Artisan provisions arrive at Harrods Food Hall — curated delicacies and ready-to-enjoy creations that bring the restaurant experience home.

2022

Climbed to No. 9

MENA's 50 Best Restaurants — Climbed to No. 9, cementing a position among the region's most celebrated dining destinations.

2023

Best Female Chef

Yasmina Hayek wins Best Female Chef award, recognizing her extraordinary culinary vision and contributions to modern Lebanese gastronomy.

2024

25+ Locations Worldwide

25+ locations across 6 countries — From Beirut to London, Doha to Muscat, Cairo to Riyadh, the brand continues its global expansion.

2025

No. 9 MENA's 50 Best

Confirmed at No. 9 on MENA's 50 Best Restaurants 2025 — a testament to unwavering commitment to excellence and Lebanese heritage.
