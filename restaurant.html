The Restaurant

A Symphony of Lebanese Elegance

Since 2011, our flagship restaurant has been the beating heart of Beirut's culinary scene.

01 Our Story

Where Every Meal Becomes a Celebration

Step into a realm of culinary splendor where Lebanese traditions unfold with a timeless touch and flair. Our flagship restaurant invites you to embark on a culinary journey, where each dish is a meticulously crafted tribute to the art of Lebanese dining.

Founded in 2011 in Beirut by Mireille Hayek, the restaurant redefined Lebanese fine dining by preserving authentic flavors while embracing modern culinary artistry. Savor the finest flavors that pay homage to the rich culinary heritage of Lebanon and immerse yourself in our uniquely created ambiance to create an unforgettable dining experience.

"Lebanese cuisine is not just food — it's the story of a civilization, told through flavors that have traveled through centuries."
Mireille Hayek Founder & President
02 Leadership

A Legacy of Passion

Mireille Hayek, Founder, opened the first Em Sherif in Beirut in 2011 and grew it into a family of Lebanese dining experiences across the Middle East and Europe. Her vision was to recreate the table of her own home, where generosity is a language and every guest is treated like family.

Yasmina Hayek carries that legacy forward and was named MENA's Best Female Chef 2025 by the region's 50 Best, bringing a refined, contemporary hand to timeless Lebanese recipes.

Together they have shaped a dining experience synonymous with Lebanese elegance across the world, from Beirut to London, Monte Carlo, Doha and Muscat.
