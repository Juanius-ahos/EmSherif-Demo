The Restaurant

A Symphony of Lebanese Elegance

Since 2011, our flagship restaurant has been the beating heart of Beirut's culinary scene.

01 Our Story

Where Every Meal Becomes a Celebration

A culinary splendor that transcends borders, Em Sherif Restaurant was founded in 2011 by the visionary Mireille Hayek. From the very first bite, guests are transported on a sensory journey through the rich tapestry of Lebanese cuisine — where tradition meets innovation, and every dish tells a story.

What began as a passion project in Beirut has grown into an international phenomenon, with locations spanning from the shores of the Mediterranean to the heart of London.

"Lebanese cuisine is not just food — it is the story of a civilization, a celebration of life itself."
Mireille Hayek Founder & President
02 A Legacy

A Legacy of Passion

Mireille Hayek opened the first Em Sherif in 2011 with a simple yet profound vision: to share the authentic flavors of Lebanese home cooking with the world. Her dedication to quality and her innate understanding of flavor transformed a small restaurant into a global brand.

Today, that legacy continues with Yasmina Hayek, who was named MENA's Best Female Chef in 2025. Under her creative direction, Em Sherif continues to push culinary boundaries while honoring the traditions that made it beloved.
