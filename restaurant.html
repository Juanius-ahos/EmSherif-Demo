The Restaurant

A Symphony of Lebanese Elegance

Since 1995, our flagship restaurant has been the beating heart of Beirut's culinary scene.

01 Our Story

Where Every Meal Becomes a Celebration

Step into a realm of culinary splendor where Lebanese traditions unfold with a timeless touch and flair. Our flagship restaurant invites you to embark on a culinary journey, where each dish is a meticulously crafted tribute to the art of Lebanese dining.

Founded in 1995 in Beirut by Mireille Hayek, the restaurant redefined Lebanese fine dining by preserving authentic flavors while embracing modern culinary artistry. Savor the finest flavors that pay homage to the rich culinary heritage of Lebanon and immerse yourself in our uniquely created ambiance to create an unforgettable dining experience.

"Lebanese cuisine is not just food — it's the story of a civilization, told through flavors that have traveled through centuries."
Mireille Hayek Founder & President
02 Leadership

A Legacy of Passion

Mireille Hayek, Founder & President, built the brand from a single Beirut restaurant into a global culinary empire. Her vision was to create a place where every moment is a celebration of culinary heritage — a place that honors the past while embracing the future.

Yasmina Hayek, Executive Chef, trained at Le Cordon Bleu, brings modern technique to timeless Lebanese recipes. Her culinary philosophy bridges tradition and innovation, creating dishes that surprise and delight while remaining rooted in authentic Lebanese flavors.

Together, they have shaped a dining experience that has become synonymous with Lebanese elegance across the world — from Beirut to London, Doha to Muscat.
