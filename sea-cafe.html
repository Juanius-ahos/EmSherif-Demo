The Sea Cafe

Where the Land
Meets the Sea

A Lebanese Mediterranean seafood restaurant paying tribute to earth and sea.

01

A Culinary Tribute
to Earth & Sea

Born from a deep reverence for the Mediterranean, The Sea Cafe is where sun-kissed coastal flavors meet the warmth of Lebanese hospitality. Every dish is crafted with the freshest ingredients — wild-caught seafood, handpicked herbs, and the finest produce from local farms.

Here, the simplicity of the sea is elevated through refined preparation and an unwavering commitment to quality. From the raw bar to the grill, each creation is a celebration of the earth's generosity and the ocean's bounty.

"The sea cafe is where Lebanon meets the Mediterranean — where the generosity of our land embraces the bounty of our waters."
— Mireille Hayek
