01

Where It All Began

In 1995, in the heart of Beirut, a visionary woman named Mireille Hayek opened the doors of a restaurant that would forever change the landscape of Lebanese cuisine. What started as a humble family kitchen grew into one of the most celebrated culinary brands in the Middle East.

Mireille's vision was simple yet revolutionary: to preserve the soul of Lebanese cooking while elevating it to the standards of world-class fine dining.

02

Cooking is an Act of Love

At the heart of everything we do is a deep respect for Lebanese culinary heritage. Every dish we serve tells the story of generations — of grandmothers who perfected recipes in their kitchens, of families who gathered around tables laden with love and abundance. We don't just cook food; we preserve a legacy.

"Cooking is an act of love — it's a way of giving, a way of sharing, a way of saying 'I care about you.' This is what Lebanese cuisine means to me."

— Mireille Hayek, Founder

The Next Chapter

Today, the brand continues under the passionate leadership of Mireille and her daughter Yasmina Hayek, who serves as Executive Chef. Yasmina, trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris, brings a fresh perspective while honoring her mother's founding vision.

Together, they have expanded the family from one restaurant to a global empire spanning three distinctive concepts — Restaurant, Café, and Deli — across seven countries.
